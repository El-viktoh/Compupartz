Standard Operating Procedure

The Compupartz 4-Stage Lab Protocol

How every device is handled from intake to final bench telemetry sign-off.

01 Process

Microscope & Rail Mapping

Full voltage impedance probing across PPBUS, 3V3, and 5V rails under high-magnification stereo microscope to pinpoint shorts without component guessing.

02 Desoldering

Ultrasonic Strip & Pad Prep

Dual-bath ultrasonic wash to neutralize liquid ingress acid. Lead-free solder removal with fine copper braid and non-corrosive synthetic flux.

03 Soldering

BGA Reballing & Placement

Direct alignment of OEM power ICs, MOSFETs, and BGA controllers using precision Japanese laser stencils and temperature-profiled infrared heating.

04 Validation

Burn-In Telemetry & 90-Day SLA

Continuous load thermal testing, power cycle loops, and full diagnostic pass before final customer pickup, backed by our 90-day comprehensive warranty.
